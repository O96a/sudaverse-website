About Us

Meet the People Behind Sudaverse

Our Story

We are Sudanese AI professionals united by a love for our country and its future. Through Sudaverse, we harness artificial intelligence to preserve heritage and unlock new opportunities across every sector of society.

SUDAVERSE mission is to preserve cultural memory, elevate our national identity, and open new pathways in art, agriculture, health, education, and technology. Together, we aim to place Sudan at the forefront of ethical and inclusive AI.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive digital ecosystem where Sudan's culture, creativity, and knowledge thrive—empowered by AI that protects our identity and advances national progress.

The Sudaverse Team

Our team brings together expertise in artificial intelligence, cultural preservation, education, and technology development. We are researchers, engineers, educators, and cultural advocates working together to ensure Sudan's voice is heard in the digital age.

Dr. Ahmed Hassan

Co-Founder & Lead AI Researcher

Dr. Hassan specializes in natural language processing and machine learning. With a Ph.D. in Computer Science and over 10 years of experience in AI research, he leads our efforts in developing culturally-aware language models for Sudanese Arabic. His passion for preserving linguistic heritage drives our core technology initiatives.

Fatima Mohamed

Co-Founder & Cultural Heritage Director

Fatima brings expertise in cultural anthropology and digital preservation. She has spent 15 years documenting Sudanese traditions, art, and oral histories. At Sudaverse, she ensures that our AI projects respect and accurately represent Sudan's rich cultural diversity while building bridges between technology and tradition.

Omar Khalid

Lead Software Engineer

Omar is a full-stack engineer with expertise in building scalable AI applications. He has worked with leading tech companies and brings 8 years of experience in software architecture. At Sudaverse, he oversees the development of our platforms, including SudaTutor, ensuring they are robust, accessible, and user-friendly.

Sara Ibrahim

Education & Community Manager

Sara is an educator with 12 years of teaching experience in Sudan. She holds a Master's in Education and is passionate about making quality learning accessible to all Sudanese students. She leads our educational initiatives, including the development and outreach of SudaTutor, ensuring it meets real classroom needs.

Dr. Yasmin Ali

Research Scientist - Computer Vision

Dr. Ali specializes in computer vision and image processing with applications in heritage preservation. Her work focuses on digitizing and analyzing historical artifacts, manuscripts, and architecture. She holds a Ph.D. from a leading international university and has published extensively on AI for cultural preservation.

Mohammed Osman

Data Science Lead

Mohammed is a data scientist with expertise in building and curating large-scale datasets. He has 7 years of experience in data engineering and machine learning. At Sudaverse, he leads our data curation efforts, ensuring that our datasets are high-quality, ethically sourced, and representative of Sudanese diversity.

Our Values

Cultural Integrity

We ensure that Sudan's cultural heritage is represented authentically and respectfully in all our AI systems.

Ethical AI

We are committed to developing AI that is fair, transparent, and beneficial to all Sudanese people.

Open Collaboration

We believe in open-source development and community participation to build AI that serves everyone.

Education First

We prioritize making quality education accessible and affordable for all Sudanese students and educators.

Innovation

We push the boundaries of AI research while staying grounded in Sudan's real-world needs and context.

Community Impact

Every project we undertake is designed to have tangible, positive impact on Sudanese communities.

Join Our Mission

We're always looking for passionate individuals who want to contribute to Sudan's digital future. Whether you're a researcher, developer, educator, cultural expert, or simply someone who cares about Sudan's heritage, there's a place for you at Sudaverse.
